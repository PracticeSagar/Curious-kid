Did You Know?

Fresh facts, every single issue

Bananas are technically berries — they get their sunny colour from a pigment called carotenoid.
Octopuses have 3 hearts and blue blood running through their veins!
Honey never spoils — archaeologists once found a 3,000-year-old jar that was still perfectly edible.
Butterflies taste their food with their feet, using tiny sensors called chemoreceptors.
📌 More Amazing Facts!
🐘 Elephants can remember familiar smells for years.
🦩 Flamingos are pink because of the shrimp and algae they eat.
🐸 Some frogs build foam nests to keep their eggs safe.
🐌 Snails can sleep for up to 3 years at a time.
🐶 Dogs have one-of-a-kind nose prints, just like human fingerprints.
